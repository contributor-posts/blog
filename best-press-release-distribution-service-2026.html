The Rest of the Field

PR Newswire is the oldest name in the industry, founded in 1954 and now owned by Cision. If your goal is legacy credibility and AP wire syndication, it delivers — but you'll need an annual membership before you can send a single release, and the pricing structure adds up quickly once you factor in word count overages and multimedia.

Business Wire, owned by Berkshire Hathaway, is the go-to for investor relations, earnings announcements, and SEC regulatory filings thanks to its direct feeds into financial terminals. It's built for a specific corporate use case, not general marketing visibility.

EIN Presswire is a solid budget option at a flat rate, with a large multilingual media contact database. It's a reasonable choice if you're comfortable writing and submitting the release yourself and don't need guaranteed placement on flagship outlets.

PRWeb leans into search visibility, with keyword optimization built into its publishing flow. It's Cision-backed, so there's institutional reliability behind it, though the more valuable syndication tiers cost more.

eReleases pairs PR Newswire's distribution network with journalist outreach and editorial review, which can be worth the higher price tag if landing genuine reporter attention — not just syndication — is the goal.

Newswire.com is built for ongoing campaigns rather than one-off announcements, with unlimited words and images per release plus a reporting dashboard, but it comes with longer contract terms.

GlobeNewswire is a specialist tool for Reg-FD compliant financial communications — not a fit if you're a small business simply trying to build brand visibility.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do press releases still help SEO in 2026?

Yes, but the value comes from the quality of the outlets that pick up your release and the resulting brand mentions and links — not from blasting a release to thousands of low-authority aggregator sites. A release published on outlets with real domain authority and organic traffic is worth far more than volume alone.

How much should a press release distribution service cost?

Pricing spans a wide range in 2026 — from under $150 for flat-rate self-serve wires to $500–$1,000+ for enterprise distribution with AP/Reuters placement. Full-service options that include professional writing, like BrandingBuzz.Agency, typically price the writing and distribution together rather than charging separately for each add-on.

Is a full-service agency better than a self-serve wire?

It depends on your resources. If you have a skilled in-house writer and just need distribution, a self-serve wire like EIN Presswire can work well. If you'd rather hand off the writing, approval, and distribution process entirely — and want media logos you can use in your own marketing — a full-service option like BrandingBuzz.Agency removes that workload.

What outlets actually matter for a press release?

Recognizable, high-traffic outlets that carry real domain authority matter far more than a long list of unfamiliar aggregator sites. Always ask a provider what specific outlets are in their network before you buy.

Can a press release help with brand trust, not just SEO?

Yes — this is where "As Seen On" media citations do real work. Being able to show potential customers that your brand was covered by recognized media outlets is a trust signal that influences buying decisions independent of any search ranking benefit.
